Safety in plain words

  • Community connections only — not bdagscan / blockdag.works.
  • Your key never leaves the wallet. Connect stays until Disconnect.
  • Stake / Unstake / Claim stay off until you lock a mining pool address.
  • If staking logic changes, sends pause until you review Contracts.
  • An admin can still change that contract later. Unstaking can take weeks. A frozen wallet cannot send.
  • Donate addresses on this site are for the community UI operator only. They are not a staking contract.

Removed here

Wrong-copy RPCs. Silent stake into unseen logic. Hidden pause status.

Still on-chain

One admin can replace logic. Freeze lists can block a wallet after it stakes.

Not claimed

We do not say safe forever. We do not invent APY. Live bars are clocks, not yield promises.
