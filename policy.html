1. What this project is

Chain-1404 Stake Manager is an unofficial, static, browser-side interface for community Chain 1404. It is published by an independent helper, not by BlockDAG Network Ltd or any related company, exchange, or “official” staking portal.

  • No custody. The operator never receives your seed, private key, or coins through this site.
  • No account. There is no signup, KYC, or hosted wallet.
  • No brokerage, fund, or deposit-taking business. You talk to public contracts through your own wallet.
  • Not staking.blockdag.engineering and not an issuer of BDAG.

You are responsible for your own compliance (including MiCA, US securities rules, AML/CFT, tax reporting, and any travel-rule duty that applies to you, not to this static site).

4. On-chain risk you accept

  • Community Chain 1404 may differ from other advertised copies of the chain. This UI refuses some official-looking RPC hosts on purpose.
  • Smart contracts can pause, upgrade, freeze an address, or fail a call after your wallet already prompted you.
  • Unstake and first-claim waits can last weeks. Adding stake while unbonding can restart a wait.
  • A wrong mining-pool address can show zero rewards or send into a pool you did not intend.
  • RPCs, explorers, and GitHub Pages can go down. Pending sends may need a separate tool such as KEDGE.
  • If your wallet is later marked blocked or restricted on-chain, this UI cannot override that.

5. Your responsibilities

  • Read every wallet confirmation. The site cannot see or stop a signature you approve.
  • Verify contract addresses against community pins on the Contracts page before you accept a migration.
  • Keep backups of your own seed offline. Nobody from this project will ask for it. If someone does, it is not us.
  • Treat Telegram @psycho_v1 as an informal contact channel, not a regulated helpdesk and not a place to send secrets.

6. Donations

Addresses on the Donate page are personal operator wallets for voluntary gifts that help keep the UI maintained. A donation is not a share, note, stake receipt, or claim on future BDAG. Send only what you can treat as a gift. Confirm the network before you press send.

7. Data on this device

The site stores theme, RPC choice, selected pool, activity log, and error text in your browser localStorage. It does not run an operator backend that collects wallets. Public RPCs you use will see the requests your browser makes. Do not paste seeds into any field.
