Staking position audit

A professional working paper for one wallet and one mining pool. It records native balance at the last shared block, live staking on the community history, live staking on the divergent history, and the network events a holder needs in order to know where they stand. Numbers come from JSON-RPC in this browser session. Narrative context is taken from the August–September 2026 community evidence papers.

This is not a court finding, not a custodian statement, and not an APY certificate. Both post-split books reuse chain ID 1404. Do not add them.
