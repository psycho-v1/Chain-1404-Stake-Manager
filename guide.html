How to use

  1. Tap Connect in the header. Pick your wallet or open this site inside a dapp browser.
  2. If asked, add BlockDAG Community (chain 1404).
  3. Paste the mining pool address you already use.
  4. Stake, unstake, or claim from Actions.
  5. Watch Insights for mix, week clock, and unbonding.
  6. If the header pill asks you to review a contract, open More → Contracts before sending more coins.
  7. Stuck send? Use KEDGE.

Timing

  • Rewards accrue after the stake confirms.
  • First claim wait is about 4 weeks from first deposit.
  • Unstake wait is about 3 weeks, then Claim.
  • Weeks follow the 7-day contract clock.
