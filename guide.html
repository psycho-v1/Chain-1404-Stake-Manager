How to use

  1. Tap Connect in the header once. The session follows you across pages until Disconnect. A short “Wallet connected” note appears and fades.
  2. If asked, add BlockDAG Community (chain 1404).
  3. Home is the overview. Actions is stake, unstake, claim and unbonding together.
  4. Detected pools show the address, a name when known, and a Most likely pool mark. Tap Use this position before any send — one pool or several, same rule.
  5. Watch Insights for mix, week clock, and unbonding bars.
  6. If the header pill asks you to review a contract, open More → Contracts before sending more coins.
  7. Feature idea or a bug? Open More → Contact and write @psycho_v1 on Telegram.
  8. Stuck send? Use KEDGE.

Timing

  • Rewards accrue after the stake confirms. The live bar is the first-claim / unbond clock, not an APY.
  • First claim wait is about 4 weeks from first deposit.
  • Unstake wait is about 3 weeks, then Claim.
  • Weeks follow the 7-day contract clock.

Before / after the split

  • Home shows three books: last shared block 316,001, live community, and GK Side (official rpc.bdagscan.com family).
  • Staking did not exist at block 316,001. Pre-split is native wallet balance only.
  • GK Side is view-only. If the wallet RPC is on GK Side, stake / unstake / claim abort with High Risk detected.
  • More → Audit builds a downloadable text record of your position and the public case chronology.

If rewards or unbonding stay at zero

  • The staking contract address is not a position. Use a listed position row — the one that already shows your yield.
  • Wrong wallet, or the position lives on a different pool.
  • A position with yield 0 is still listed if principal is there. Adding more to a zero-yield position does not move coins to a position that already pays.
  • RPC still checking — wait for Network OK in the header.
