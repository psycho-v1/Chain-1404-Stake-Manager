How to use

  1. Tap Connect in the header. Pick your wallet or open this site inside a dapp browser. It stays connected until you tap Disconnect.
  2. If asked, add BlockDAG Community (chain 1404).
  3. Paste the mining pool address you already stake with — not only the community staking proxy. Search, then tap Lock to engage.
  4. Home then streams staked / rewards / unbonding every 12 seconds.
  5. Stake, unstake, or claim from Actions. Buttons stay off until a pool is locked and the contract allows the send.
  6. Watch Insights for mix, week clock, and unbonding bars.
  7. If the header pill asks you to review a contract, open More → Contracts before sending more coins.
  8. Feature idea or a bug? Open More → Contact and write @psycho_v1 on Telegram.
  9. Stuck send? Use KEDGE.

Timing

  • Rewards accrue after the stake confirms. The live bar is the first-claim / unbond clock, not an APY.
  • First claim wait is about 4 weeks from first deposit.
  • Unstake wait is about 3 weeks, then Claim.
  • Weeks follow the 7-day contract clock.

If rewards or unbonding stay at zero

  • You locked the staking proxy instead of your mining pool. Search the pool 0x and lock that.
  • Wrong wallet, or the position lives on a different pool.
  • RPC still checking — wait for Network OK in the header.
